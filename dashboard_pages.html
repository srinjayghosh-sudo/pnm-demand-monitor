    Definitions

    Google Trends score
    0–100 per state. 100 = the busiest week for these keywords in that state; 50 = half as many searches. A relative measure, not a search count.
    Demand now
    Last 7 days of the score vs its 12-month average.
    Next 4 weeks
    Expected demand vs a normal week, from 5 years of seasonality. Back-tested on the past year.
    Impression share
    Share of eligible searches where an ad was shown (last 14 days).
    Lost to budget
    Eligible impressions missed because the daily budget was exhausted.
    Lost to rank
    Eligible impressions missed due to bid or ad quality (Ad Rank).
    CPA
    Cost incl. GST per conversion, last 30 days (conversions older than 7 days).
    Rental searches
    “house for rent”, “rent agreement”, “flat for rent”: a leading indicator of moves.
    Confidence
    High: outlook back-tested within ±10% with full delivery data. Medium: partial data. Low: limited data.
