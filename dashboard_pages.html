    Search trend by keyword

    What the numbers mean: Google Trends does not give search counts. It gives a 0–100 score for each state: 100 = the busiest week for these keywords in that state, 50 = half as many searches as that week. So “packers and movers: 51” means about half the searches of the peak week, and “house shifting: 3.9” means it gets roughly 13× fewer searches than packers and movers. Switch to “Each vs its own average” to compare the shape of small keywords.

    What the terms mean

    Google Trends score
    0–100 per state. 100 = the busiest week for these keywords in that state; 50 = half as many searches. A relative measure, not a search count.
    Demand now
    Last 7 days of the score vs its 12-month average.
    Next 4 weeks
    Expected demand vs a normal week, from 5 years of seasonality. Back-tested on the past year.
    Impression share
    Of the searches you were eligible for, the share where your ad showed (last 14 days).
    Lost to budget
    Share missed because the daily budget ran out. High → raise budget.
    Lost to rank
    Share missed because bid or ad quality was too low. High → raise bids/tCPA or improve ads.
    CPA
    Cost incl. GST per conversion, last 30 matured days (conversions need ~7 days to settle).
    Rental searches
    “house for rent”, “rent agreement”, “flat for rent”: usually rise before moves.
    Confidence
    High = outlook tested within ±10% and delivery data present; Medium = partial data; Low = little data yet.
